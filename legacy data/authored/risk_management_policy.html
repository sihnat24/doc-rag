Risk Management Policy

Policy Number: PM-RISK-002  |  Version: 3.1  |  Effective Date: January 15, 2023

1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for identifying, assessing, tracking, and mitigating risks across all active programs within the Modernization Equipment Acquisition (MEA) Program Office. Consistent risk management practices ensure that program leadership maintains situational awareness of threats to cost, schedule, and technical performance, and that appropriate escalation and response actions are taken in a timely manner.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all MEA Program Office personnel and to all contractors performing work under MEA program contracts. Contractors are required to maintain a program-level risk register and report risk status updates to the Contracting Officer's Representative (COR) no less than quarterly, or immediately upon identification of a new High risk item.

3. Risk Status Definitions

All program risks shall be assigned one of three status designations based on the combined assessment of likelihood and impact. Status designations drive reporting requirements and escalation thresholds as described in Section 5.

3.1 Green

A Green risk status indicates that no significant risk has been identified, or that identified risks have been fully mitigated and retired. Programs or subsystems carrying a Green status require no immediate action. Green status shall be reported in the quarterly program status brief with a brief narrative confirming continued health. A subsystem may carry a Green risk status even if minor issues are being monitored, provided those issues do not threaten cost, schedule, or technical performance within the current reporting period.

3.2 Yellow

A Yellow risk status indicates that a risk has been identified that has the potential to impact cost, schedule, or technical performance if not actively managed. Yellow risks require a documented mitigation plan with assigned ownership and a target retirement date. The mitigation plan shall be submitted to the COR within 30 calendar days of the risk being elevated to Yellow status. Yellow risks shall be briefed at each quarterly program status review. A risk that has been at Yellow status for two consecutive quarters without demonstrated progress toward retirement shall be considered for escalation to Red.

3.3 Red

A Red risk status indicates that a risk has materialized or is highly likely to materialize, with confirmed or near-certain impact to cost, schedule, or technical performance. Red status requires immediate written notification to the Program Manager and Contracting Officer. A corrective action plan (CAP) with specific, time-bound milestones shall be submitted within 15 calendar days of Red designation. Red risks shall be briefed at all program reviews until retired. If a contractor's performance is the primary driver of a Red risk, the Contracting Officer shall assess whether a cure notice or show cause letter is warranted under the terms of the contract.

4. Risk Likelihood and Impact Definitions

Risk likelihood shall be assessed as Low, Medium, or High based on the probability of occurrence within the current program phase. Risk impact shall be assessed as Low, Medium, or High based on the consequences to cost, schedule, or technical performance if the risk were to materialize.

A risk with High likelihood and High impact shall always carry a Red status designation. A risk with Medium likelihood and High impact, or High likelihood and Medium impact, shall carry a Yellow status designation. All other combinations may be designated Green at the program manager's discretion, provided a monitoring plan is documented.

5. Escalation Thresholds and Reporting Requirements

Risk status changes shall be reported as follows. A change from Green to Yellow requires notification to the COR within five business days and inclusion in the next quarterly status report. A change from Yellow to Red requires immediate written notification to the Program Manager, Contracting Officer, and Deputy Program Manager, followed by a CAP within 15 calendar days. A change from Red to Yellow or Green (risk retirement or significant mitigation) shall be documented in the risk register with a narrative description of the action taken and approved by the Program Manager before the status change is recorded as final.

6. Risk Register Requirements

Each program and subsystem shall maintain a risk register capturing, at minimum: a unique risk identifier, a plain-language description of the risk, the subsystem or contract affected, the current likelihood and impact ratings, the current status designation (Green/Yellow/Red), the name of the risk owner, the planned mitigation approach, the target retirement date, and a notes field documenting status history. The risk register shall be updated at least quarterly and submitted as an attachment to the quarterly program status brief.

7. Cure Notices

When a contractor's failure to perform is a primary or contributing cause of a Red risk designation, the Contracting Officer shall evaluate whether a cure notice is appropriate. A cure notice formally notifies the contractor that their performance is endangering contract performance and establishes a period — typically 10 to 30 days — within which the contractor must demonstrate adequate progress toward resolution. Failure to satisfy a cure notice may result in termination for default. Issuance of a cure notice does not obligate the Government to terminate the contract and does not preclude the parties from continuing to work toward resolution.

8. Policy Compliance

Compliance with this policy is the responsibility of all MEA Program Office staff and contractors. Questions regarding interpretation or application of this policy should be directed to the Deputy Program Manager or the MEA Program Office Policy Coordinator.
